# Remarketing Planner

Plan a 6-month email + SMS remarketing calendar for loan leads, **front-loaded in the first 24 hours**, and estimate net revenue, ROI, unsubscribes and lifetime value by segment and strategy. Model version **0.2.0**.

## The problem
Leads that do not convert immediately are usually contacted ad hoc. Intent decays quickly after the first action, but over-contacting burns the list. The planner turns a schedule into expected conversions, costs and unsubscribes so calendars can be compared before sending anything.

## Results (cohort of 10,000 leads, hypothetical parameters)
Main segment (`applied`, 21 touches): net **9,808 EUR**, ROI 2.6x, LTV per lead 11.00 -> 11.98 EUR, **61% of incremental revenue within 24 h**.

| segment | touches | net EUR (front-loaded) | ROI | LTV EUR | revenue <= 24 h | net, weekly email | net, aggressive daily |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `applied` | 21 | 9,808 | 2.56 | 11.98 | 61% | 4,892 | 9,979 |
| `first` | 20 | 9,725 | 2.54 | 6.97 | 68% | 5,295 | 8,573 |
| `fail` | 15 | -185 | -0.07 | 1.98 | 46% | 932 | -1,358 |
| `asnef` | 15 | 646 | 0.22 | 3.06 | 53% | 1,349 | -44 |
| `hq` | 18 | 28,096 | 8.37 | 20.81 | 64% | 14,646 | 36,438 |
| `cold` | 16 | -581 | -0.17 | 0.94 | 35% | 1,890 | -657 |

The front-loaded design beats a weekly email in every profitable segment; the aggressive daily plan can earn slightly more net profit in `applied` and `hq` but unsubscribes far more leads, so front-loading is a robust compromise rather than a uniform winner. Loss-making segments (`fail`, `cold`) should be contacted less or not at all.

## Model and assumptions
Per touch at day *t*: intent `w(t) = floor + (1-floor) exp(-t/tau)`, fatigue `1/(1 + fat * touches in the last 48 h)`, conversion `alive * r_channel * w * fatigue`, unsubscribes `alive * u_channel * (1 + ufat * touches in the last 48 h)`, incremental revenue `conversions * V * incrementality` (0.7). Costs 0.003 EUR per email and 0.05 EUR per SMS. Details in the paper.

**All parameters are hypotheses**, listed in [`data/PARAMETERS.md`](data/PARAMETERS.md). There is no dataset and therefore **no backtest**: the tests verify internal consistency (cohort conservation, independent recomputation of a touch, monotonicity in the drivers, strategy ranking, JS/Python parity), not predictive accuracy. Sensitivity (+-25%): response and value move net profit by about +-3,300 EUR, unsubscribe rates by under 200 EUR.

## Known limits
- Response rates, decay times, value per conversion and incrementality are not estimated from data; replace them with holdout results.
- Fatigue and unsubscribe effects use a simple 48-hour window; channel interactions and deliverability are ignored.
- Segments are independent: a lead that converts in one flow is not removed from the others.
- LTV is the base value plus net flow profit per lead, not a full customer lifetime model.
